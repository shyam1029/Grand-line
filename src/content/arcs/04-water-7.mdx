## The city built like a fountain

Water 7 rises out of the sea in rings, with a great fountain at its crown and canals for streets. Nobody walks far here; they ride Yagara Bulls, long-necked sea horses that haul boats through the waterways.

The island's trade is ships. The Galley-La Company's docks will repair anything that still floats — and will tell a crew, without sentiment, when a keel is beyond saving.

### Navigator's notes

- A week in port is enough for the Log to set, and long enough for a full refit.
- Once a year **Aqua Laguna** arrives: a storm tide tall enough to drown the lower city. When the bells ring, move the ship to high ground or lose it.
- The **Sea Train** runs on rails laid across the open sea, linking the island to its neighbours and to the government's judicial island beyond.

### A ship's last harbour

This is where voyages are measured honestly. A crew that arrives proud of how far its ship has carried it may leave having learned how much that journey cost — and what it means to say goodbye to a companion made of timber.

> A ship that brings its crew this far has already done more than anyone asked of it.
