## A kingdom running out of rain

Alabasta is one of the oldest civilisations on the Grand Line: a sea of dunes cut by the Sandora River, ruled from the plateau city of Alubarna. For three years the rain has fallen only on the capital. Everywhere else the wells are filling with sand, and a rebel army has stopped believing it is an accident.

It isn't. Baroque Works has been salting the sky with Dance Powder and letting the crown take the blame.

### Navigator's notes

- Approach from the Sandora delta and make port at **Nanohana**. The outer reefs are shallow, shifting and unmarked.
- Sandstorms blow off the interior without warning. Reef the sails early and keep the Log Pose under cloth.
- The island's field is slow to release a needle. Plan provisions for a long stay before the Log sets for the next island.

### Across the sand

The road inland runs through the dried-out oasis of Yuba to **Rainbase**, where a casino shaped like a great banana-gator hides the kingdom's most dangerous guest, and on to the clock tower square in Alubarna where the war is meant to end — one way or another.

> Somewhere under all that sand, someone is still digging for water.
