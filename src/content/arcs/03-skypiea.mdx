## When the needle points at the sky

Sometimes a Log Pose stops pointing at the horizon and tilts upward instead. The instrument is not broken. A stronger field has overwritten the record, and the island that owns it is overhead.

Skypiea floats on the White-White Sea, a layer of island cloud dense enough to sail on. Its people trade in Dials — shells that store wind, heat and impact — and treat a handful of ordinary soil as treasure.

### Navigator's notes

- The only fast road up is the **Knock Up Stream**: a monstrous vertical current that erupts from the sea floor. Ride it at the centre or be thrown clear.
- Fit the ship for flight before the eruption. There is no second attempt.
- Up top, sea-cloud carries a keel but island-cloud does not give way. Chart both.

### The Upper Yard

Four hundred years ago the same current tore a piece of the island of Jaya out of the sea and lodged it in the clouds. The Shandia have fought to reclaim that stolen ground ever since. At its heart grows Giant Jack, a beanstalk tall enough to reach a ruined city of gold — and a bell whose ring was promised to someone waiting far below.

> If the bell rings, the people on the sea will know the city was never a lie.
