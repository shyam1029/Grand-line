## An island that isn't one

Sabaody is not rock at all. It is a forest of Yarukiman mangroves whose roots rise out of the sea and knit together into ground. Because there is no island beneath it, there is no magnetic field to overwrite a Log Pose — a crew can stay as long as it likes and the needle will not move.

The roots breathe out a natural resin that swells into bubbles. People here ride them, live in them, and build amusement parks out of them.

### Navigator's notes

- The seven routes of Paradise converge here. Expect every notable rookie crew to be in port at the same time.
- The Red Line cannot be sailed over. The way on is *under* it: have the ship **coated** in resin by a craftsman and dive ten thousand metres to the passage below.
- Coating takes days. Choose the craftsman carefully and stay out of trouble until it is done.

### Lawless and lawful groves

Marine Headquarters sits just across the water, and the World Nobles shop in the upper groves. Draw their attention and an admiral arrives within hours. The lower-numbered groves, meanwhile, belong to bounty hunters, kidnappers and an auction house that sells people.

> Every crew reaches Sabaody thinking it has seen the sea. This is where the sea corrects them.
