## The country behind the waterfall

Wano does not want to be found. The whole nation sits atop sheer cliffs, and the only way in from the sea is to climb the waterfall that pours off them — hauled upstream by giant koi, through a storm that never clears.

Inside is a country that has kept its borders shut for centuries: six regions, a capital in permanent blossom, and swordsmen strong enough that even the Marines keep their distance.

### Navigator's notes

- This is the **New World**. A standard Log Pose is no longer enough; carry the three-needle model and trust the steadiest one.
- No needle records Wano. Make the approach with an Eternal Pose or a Vivre Card, and expect to lose the ship's heading in the squall off the coast.
- Do not attempt the falls without something very large pulling the bow.

### Twenty years of waiting

Wano's rightful rulers were cut down a generation ago, and the country has been stripped for its factories ever since. Everything here turns on a single night: a raid on the island fortress of Onigashima, timed to a festival, by people who have been waiting two decades to keep their word.

> The borders were closed to protect something. Opening them is the last promise left to keep.
